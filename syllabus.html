About the BPUT Syllabus

Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT) publishes an official syllabus for every branch and semester, covering the subjects, credit structure and exam scheme students need to plan their studies. For most batches, the 1st year syllabus is common across all engineering branches — CSE, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Mining, Metallurgical and Mineral Engineering students all follow the same 1st and 2nd semester document. From the 3rd semester onward, subjects diverge by branch.

Use the finder above to jump straight to your branch and semester — each result links directly to the official PDF, and if your branch shares that semester's syllabus with other departments, we'll show you which ones.

❓ FAQ

Common questions about the BPUT syllabus

Is the BPUT syllabus PDF free to download?

Yes. Every syllabus on this page is free to view and download as a PDF via Google Drive — no login, no payment, no waiting timers.

Is the 1st year BPUT syllabus the same for every branch?

For most admission batches, BPUT runs a common 1st year syllabus across all engineering branches, so CSE, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and other departments share the same document. From 2nd year onward, the syllabus becomes branch-specific.

Which BPUT branches and semesters are covered here?

Computer Science (CSE), Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Mining, Metallurgical and Mineral Engineering, across all 8 semesters.
